The first sharded Athena pass does not reproduce the earlier Modal tie. The conventional control
reaches 449/500 (89.8%), while the same rational χ checkpoint reaches 426/500 (85.2%), a 4.6-point
gap. The shards were matched by task but split across A6000 and A30 GPUs. On A6000-assigned tasks,
χ is 9/250 successes ahead. On A30-assigned tasks, it is 32/250 behind. Because this reveals an
architecture-by-hardware confound, jobs 830528–830535 rerun every task for both architectures on
A6000 GPUs. The held A30 matrix tests whether the interaction replicates. The paper must not use the
earlier exact-tie claim unless a controlled same-GPU result supports it.

The conventional model has 20,138,632 parameters and the χ model has 20,137,352, a difference of
1,280 parameters (0.006%). On one A30 and fixed batch of 128, the χ training step is 3.12 times
slower and uses 1.63 times the peak allocated memory of the conventional twin.

The first same-A30 inference profile measures 38.32 ms for χ and 7.23 ms for the conventional
twin, a 5.30-times eager gap. `torch.compile` failed because Athena's home quota was full, so jobs
830544 and 830545 redirect Triton and Inductor caches to `/work` and rerun on A30 and A6000. The
quota failure is retained as `inference_profile_pair_quota_failure.json`.
